PyGuide Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

English

PyGuide is an educational reference app for Python. We do not require an account and do not intentionally collect names, contacts, precise location, or user-created content.

The app may process limited technical information needed to operate advertising, such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, diagnostics, and advertising interaction data. Advertising is provided by Unity Ads and is subject to Unity's privacy policy. You can manage advertising and tracking permissions in your device settings.

Learning progress is stored locally on your device. We do not sell personal information. We retain technical information only as long as needed for service operation, security, measurement, and legal obligations.

For privacy questions or requests, contact rebichoco@gmail.com. This policy may be updated by publishing a revised version on this page.
